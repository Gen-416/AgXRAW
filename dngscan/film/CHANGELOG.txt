What ships here
---------------
dngscan/data/film/observe_v1.json (schema agxraw-film-observe/1) does not
contain the spektrafilm profiles themselves. Its per-stock entries are
quantities computed from them, and are therefore distributed under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 terms:

  - curve:       AgX C1 curve parameters least-squares fitted to the neutral
                 scale of a spectral simulation built on the spektrafilm film
                 and print profiles (negative -> paper, or reversal viewed
                 directly), under native viewing with luminance black-point
                 compensation and EV0 -> 0.18.
  - exposure_offset_ev (reversal stocks): ISO 2240 metered mid grey
                 measured on that simulated neutral scale.
  - hue:         Fourier coefficients of a per-hue Oklab rendition fitted to
                 the spectral simulation's output on 526 synthetic scene
                 colours.
  - crossover:   the simulation's per-channel grey-scale cast table.
  - stage_a:     a 3x3 layer-exposure observer fitted to the profile's
                 spectral sensitivities, and the characteristic (exposure ->
                 dye amount) tables resampled from the profile's density
                 curves.

Not derived from spektrafilm (listed in NOTICE.md): the grain sigma(D)
tables (digitized Kodak 5207 granularity chart), the per-stock grain scales
(Kodak / Fujifilm datasheet granularity figures), the grain field model.
